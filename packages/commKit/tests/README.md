# CommKit Tests

The CommKit suite covers:

- chunking: 254 bytes as one chunk, 255 and 256 as two, 502 as two full chunks, 503 and 510 as three, the exact header bytes of each, consecutive stream ids, two-digit chunk numbers past 127, and no forbidden byte in any chunk;
- reassembly: single chunks, the logged channel, a loopback of what CommKit sent, no partial delivery, chunks out of order, interleaved streams from one sender, one stream id kept apart across senders and distributions, expiry of a stream with a missing chunk reported once, the timeout counted from the last chunk, chunks for unknown streams, hostile first chunks refused before anything is stored, streams dropped for an out-of-range index, a duplicate, a short middle or an early last chunk, every reassembly bound, a restarted stream id, eviction on `GROUP_ROSTER_UPDATE` (also by the name before the realm), foreign prefixes and unknown control bytes, secret arguments, callback isolation, and streams discarded with the last registration;
- queues: `tooLarge`, `queueFull` by bytes and by messages, lowering a bound, every forbidden byte, `badDistribution` for unknown distributions and missing targets, ignored targets, `closed`, logged sends, `unavailable`, depth per priority, refusal counters, and no handler, job or timer left once idle;
- priorities: the ALERT, NORMAL, BULK rotation, BULK never starved, round-robin between destinations, chunks interleaved with other destinations, and order inside one destination;
- budget: the initial bucket, the per-message charge, waiting for the bucket, the zoning mode, the low-frame-rate mode sampled on a timer and never while idle, a chunk above a small burst, and limit validation;
- outside traffic with HookKit loaded: addon, logged and chat messages charged once, CommKit's own sends not charged, and debt down to two seconds; without HookKit nothing is charged;
- throttling: a throttled pipe set aside while others continue, doubling backoff reset by a success, a long message resumed at the refused chunk, failures named from `Enum`, and a send function that raises;
- send handles and scopes: progress and completion, cancel while queued, mid-send and from a progress callback, isolated completion callbacks, `CancelAll`, `Close` mid-send, shutdown mid-send through LifecycleKit, a scope requested after shutdown, `CloseAddonScopes`;
- registrations: one client registration per prefix, prefixes the client already holds, `DuplicatePrefix` and every refusal, the 32-registration bound, disconnection, the chat events connected only while needed, and `UnregisterAll`;
- SyncSet: FNV-1a hash vectors of the canonical encoding, insertion-order independence, refused values, clearing, delta replies, acks, removed fields, the reply interval, deliveries and `OnChanged`, requests carrying held hashes, a multi-chunk round trip between two SyncSets, malformed frames, undeclared fields, the peer cache bound, the listener bound, closing; with SchemaKit, schema refusals of received and local values and invalid schemas; without CodecKit or SchemaKit, the refusals at the caller;
- statistics after a mixed scenario, and fresh tables;
- allocation guards (`collectgarbage("count")` with the collector stopped) on the single-chunk receive path, a foreign prefix, and the queries;
- duplicate embedded loading, Registry publication, loading without the optional packages, `securecallfunction` isolation, yielding to a newer revision, an in-place upgrade to revision 2 with a send in flight, an open stream, a registration and a SyncSet, a missing Registry, each missing required package, and an incomplete facade;
- `error` levels: every receiver, argument, option, limit and secret failure reports the caller's own line;
- manifest/runtime API and revision consistency, the six required and three optional dependencies, and `optionalDependencies` after the top-level `api`.

`support/CommKitTestEnv.lua` loads Registry, SignalKit, EventKit, LifecycleKit, TimerKit, SchedulerKit, PoolKit, CodecKit and CommKit on the shared fixture, and HookKit or SchemaKit on request; CodecKit, HookKit and SchemaKit are declared under `optionalDependencies`, so the runner puts them on `LUA_PATH`. It stubs `C_ChatInfo` with a recorded outbox, every attempt recorded and scripted results; `Enum`; `GetFramerate`; `UnitInParty` and `UnitInRaid`; and, for the HookKit chain, `hooksecurefunc`. `Deliver` and `DeliverLogged` emit the chat events through the fixture, and `Loopback` delivers the outbox back as if from a peer.

The fixture's timers fire only when a spec fires them, so the environment wraps `C_Timer` before TimerKit binds it and records when each native timer is due on the fixture clock. `Advance(seconds)` moves the clock, fires every timer that falls due in order and runs the scheduler's frame driver after each; `Advance(0)` runs what is due now, which is how the driver job and SchedulerKit's next-frame timers run.
